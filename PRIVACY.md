# Privacy Policy

**Indige** · Effective date: 11/13/2026 · Last updated: 11/13/2026
**Developer:** Kenny Solorio · **Contact:** indigeapp@duck.com

## 1. Summary

Indige is designed to work without collecting personal information. It has no user accounts and no server, and the app doesn't transmit your data. All content and any data you create stays on your device.

## 2. Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

- personal information such as name, email address, or phone number
- location data
- advertising or device identifiers
- usage analytics or behavioral data
- contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data

## 3. Information stored on your device

| Data | Where it lives | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary content | Local database bundled with the app | To provide the dictionary |
| Favorite words  | Local database on your device | To save your favorites and build practice sets |
| Practice progress  | Local storage on your device | To track your flashcard practice |

This data is private to the app on your device and isn't accessible to me.

## 4. Sharing

I don't share, sell, or transfer any user data, because none is collected. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking components. I will verify at release.

## 5. Third-party platforms

Downloading the app through Google Play is governed by Google's privacy policy. Your device's operating system may also back up or process app data under its own settings.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. Uninstalling the app deletes the data it stored locally.

## 7. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age required in your region).

## 8. AI

Indige contains no AI features and doesn't send any data to AI services. AI tools were used during development of the app's code; see [AI_POLICY.md](AI_POLICY.md).

## 9. Changes to this policy

I'll post updates here and revise the date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
